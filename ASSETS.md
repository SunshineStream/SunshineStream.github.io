# Asset sources

- `assets/sunshine.svg` and `assets/social.jpg` come from
  [SunshineStream/Sunshine](https://github.com/SunshineStream/Sunshine), at `sunshine.svg`
  and `branding/github/banner.jpg`.
  These assets retain their original GPL-3.0 licensing; a copy is included at `LICENSES/GPL-3.0.txt`.
- `assets/sunshine-web-ui-light.jpg` and `assets/sunshine-web-ui-dark.jpg` are captures of the welcome screen
  built from Sunshine at
  [a5265bc](https://github.com/SunshineStream/Sunshine/tree/a5265bc4abddc8915773cfa6bceaf6f2ca660608/src_assets/common/assets/web).
  They use Sunshine's default Light and Dark themes and retain its GPL-3.0 licensing.
  The hero's device-and-canyon composition is a separate illustration.
- `assets/lizardbyte.svg` comes from
  [LizardByte's branding directory](https://github.com/LizardByte/.github/tree/master/branding/logos).
  The LizardByte logo was created by GitHub user [BenDev9](https://github.com/BenDev9).
- `assets/landscape.svg` is an original vector illustration for this website.
  It is covered by this website's AGPL-3.0-only license.
- Manrope is bundled locally from [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope).
  Its SIL Open Font License is included at `assets/fonts/OFL-Manrope.txt`.

Product and organization names and logos identify their respective projects. Brand ownership is unchanged.
The website's AGPL license does not replace the existing asset and font terms.
